# Nutrition intake and plan checklist

## Estimated calorie target

Collect once when absent:

- Age, sex, current weight, height, or a measured BMR.
- Primary objective and preferred rate of change.
- Weekly training/activity pattern.
- Relevant allergies/intolerances and only safety context necessary for this request. Do not demand a full clinical history, lab panel, or medication list for a simple meal suggestion.

If activity is incomplete, choose a conservative activity assumption, label it, and provide a provisional target plus a review point after 2–3 weeks of weight trend, waist measurement, hunger, training performance, and adherence.

## Practical meal structure

For each meal, set:

1. A protein anchor.
2. A carbohydrate portion scaled to the calorie target and training timing.
3. A vegetable/fruit option the user tolerates.
4. A low-effort preparation method and one inexpensive substitution.

## Daily tracking

- Log only what was actually consumed, not food merely ordered or available.
- Estimate ranges when portions, oils, sauces, or product labels are unknown.
- State the current-day total only from foods reported in that day/context.
- Track only on request in the current conversation; reset totals for a new day. Save no meals or preferences to persistent memory/files without specific authorization for content and destination.
